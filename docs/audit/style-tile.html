BMW X3 (2003–2008): EWS4

Method BENCH · Tools: AK90, Tango · Source: book p. 16 rights to confirm

Vehicle and ECU
BrandBMW
ModelX3
Years2003–2008
ModuleEWS4
Target device2L86D

Bench and wiring steps

  1. Remove the lower kick panel on the driver’s side.
  2. Locate the EWS4 module on the driver’s side ‘A’ pillar, 6 to 10 inches straight up.
  3. Identify the module by its white label with black writing (EWS3 has green text).
  4. Unscrew the single 8 mm or 10 mm nut securing the module.
  5. Read the 2L86D in-circuit using the AK90 reader or Tango programmer.

Safety: confirm the module first. EWS4 has a white label with black writing; EWS3 has green text.
