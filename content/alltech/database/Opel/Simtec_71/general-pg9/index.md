## Programming Instructions

**Method:** `BENCH`

### Bench / Wiring Steps

1. SIMTEC 71 (Kline), SIMTEC 71.1 (Kline), SIMTEC 71.5 (CAN), SIMTEC 71.6 (CAN):
2. Connect +12V to Pins 31 and 32
3. Connect +12V IGNITION to Pin 64
4. Connect GND to ECU Case
5. Connect CANH to Pin 38
6. Connect CANL to Pin 6
7. Connect KLINE to Pin 5
8. SIMTEC 75 (CAN), SIMTEC 77 (CAN), SIMTEC 78 (CAN):
9. Connect +12V to Pins 31 and 32
10. Connect +12V IGNITION to Pin 64
11. Connect GND to ECU Case
12. Connect CANH to Pin 38
13. Connect CANL to Pin 6
