## Programming Instructions

**Method:** `BENCH`

### Bench / Wiring Steps

1. For BMW MINI/ROVER Siemens EMS2 / EMS5150 (FLASH 29F400BB): Connect +12V power to pins 118, 120, and 121.
2. For BMW MINI/ROVER Siemens EMS2 / EMS5150: Connect K-Line to pin 117.
3. For BMW MINI/ROVER Siemens EMS2 / EMS5150: Connect Ground (GND) to pins 2 and 116.
4. For GM Siemens/Continental D42: Connect +12V power to pins 29 and 30.
5. For GM Siemens/Continental D42: Connect K-Line to pin 56.
6. For GM Siemens/Continental D42: Connect Ground (GND) to pin 28.
