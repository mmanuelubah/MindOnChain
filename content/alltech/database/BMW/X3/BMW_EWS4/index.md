## Programming Instructions

**Method:** `BENCH`

### Bench / Wiring Steps

> **Required Tool:** AK90, Tango

1. Remove the lower kick panel on the driver's side.
2. Locate the EWS4 module on the driver's side 'A' pillar, 6 to 10 inches straight up.
3. Identify the module by looking for a white label with black writing (EWS3 has green text).
4. Unscrew the single 8mm or 10mm nut securing the EWS4 module.
5. Read the 2L86D target device in-circuit using the AK90 reader or Tango programmer.

---

### Safety Notes

> **Warning:** Ensure module identification is correct: EWS4 uses a white label with black writing, whereas EWS3 uses green text.
>
