How this site is made and tended.

Set in Inter and Departure Mono, this site is built with Hugo, hosted on Vercel, and optimized for speed and readability. It was tested with accessibility in mind, ensuring it works well for everyone.

I don’t write code often, but working closer to the browser helps me design with greater accessibility and intention. I’ve used AI for debugging, exploring ideas, and early drafts. Still, every line is refined by me.

The light and dark switch on the navigation bar is a bulb on a rope. Turn it on and the page shouts “Up NEPA!” Turn it off and it just groans. NEPA was the National Electric Power Authority, the body in charge of Nigeria’s electricity, and lack thereof. “Up NEPA!” is what every Nigerian child yelled when the power came back.

If you find anything that doesn’t work well for you, send me an email.
