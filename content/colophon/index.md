How this site is made and tended.

Set in Archivo, Pixelify Sans and Source Serif 4, this site is built with Hugo and hosted on Vercel. The home page weighs about 270 KB and scores 100 for accessibility in Lighthouse on a throttled phone; every page is checked at phone width and at 200% zoom.

I don’t write code often, but working closer to the browser helps me design with greater accessibility and intention. I’ve used AI for debugging, exploring ideas, and early drafts. Still, every line is refined by me.

The light and dark switch on the navigation bar is a bulb on a rope. Turn it on and the page lights up with “Up NEPA!” Turn it off and the screen flickers out: “Damn, they’ve taken the light.” NEPA was the National Electric Power Authority, the body in charge of Nigeria’s electricity, and lack thereof. “Up NEPA!” is what every Nigerian child yelled when the power came back.

If you find anything that doesn’t work well for you, send me an email.
