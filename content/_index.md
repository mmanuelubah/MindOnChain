  - q: "My replacement ECU is fitted but the car still won’t start. Why?"
    a: "A replacement ECU has to be programmed to your car before the engine will run: either cloned from your old ECU, or virginised and then matched to the immobiliser. Until that is done, the immobiliser blocks the start."
  - q: "What does ECU cloning mean?"
    a: "Copying the data from your old engine computer to a replacement, so the car accepts the new ECU and starts without a dealer visit."
  - q: "What is ECU virginising (wiping)?"
    a: "Returning a used ECU to a blank state so it can be programmed to a different car."
  - q: "Can you find an ECU or module at Ladipo Market for me?"
