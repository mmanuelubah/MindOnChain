CAN bus, explained

What does a car’s CAN bus actually say?

Every module in a modern car talks over two wires. Here is one real message, read off a scope.

Two wires, mirrored

CAN-H and CAN-L both rest at 2.5 V. For each “0” bit, CAN-H rises to 3.5 V and CAN-L falls to 1.5 V.

CAN-H
CAN-L

The ECU reads the difference, so noise that hits both wires cancels out.
