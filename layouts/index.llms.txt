# {{ site.Title }}

> {{ site.Params.description }}

{{ site.Title }} is an automotive locksmith and ECU workshop in {{ site.Params.location }}, run by {{ site.Params.author }}, an automobile locksmith and technician. It programs, clones and virginises engine control units (ECUs), works on immobiliser systems, sources tested modules from Ladipo Market in Lagos, and publishes the procedures in a free online database.

- Location: {{ site.Params.location }}. Parts are collected in Lagos; delivery elsewhere is arranged on request.
- Contact: WhatsApp +{{ $c.whatsapp }} ({{ $c.whatsappDisplay }}) · {{ $c.email }}
- Prices: on request, because Ladipo Market prices change; a price and photos of the exact part are sent before the customer pays anything.
- Modules are tested on the bench before handover.
